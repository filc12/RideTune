Thank you. Below are the exact steps to reach the in-app purchase.

There is no restriction by storefront, region or device configuration, and no account or
login is required. The app is iPhone-only and does not declare iPad support, so on an iPad
it runs in iPhone compatibility mode; the flow below is identical on both.

FASTEST ROUTE

1. Launch the app. Complete the short onboarding: type any name, tap Next, set any rider
   weight, tap Next.
2. On the Home screen, tap the blue button "Choose bike" and select any motorcycle
   (for example: BMW, then R 1250 GS).
3. Stay on the Home screen and scroll to the row of load chips labelled
   "Solo", "With luggage", "2 riders", "2 riders + luggage".
4. Tap "With luggage" or "2 riders".
5. The purchase sheet opens immediately, showing the title "RideTune Premium Lifetime",
   the price, "Restore purchases" and "Maybe later".

ALTERNATIVE ROUTES — each opens the same purchase sheet

- Bottom tab "Load": set "Passenger" or "Luggage" above 0 kg.
- Home screen, "Saved setups": try to save a setup.
- Bottom tab "Diagnostics": tap the locked card.
- "Choose bike": try to add a second motorcycle.

PRODUCT DETAILS

Identifier: ridetune_premium_lifetime. Non-consumable, one-time purchase granting lifetime
access; not a subscription. It was submitted together with this version and appears as
"Ready for Review" in App Store Connect. The Paid Apps Agreement is active in the Business
section (effective 07/08/2026).
